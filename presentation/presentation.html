Industry Context & Motivation

The Challenge Facing Engineering Students

Curriculum vs Industry Gap

Students face profound ambiguity about which programming languages and toolsets are in highest demand by hiring managers.

Subjective Career Advice

Advice from blogs or social media is unverified, noisy, and often pushes short-term hype over structural demand.

Messy Recruitment Data

Job postings feature conflicting abbreviations (py vs Python), multi-city tags, and unstandardized job titles.

Fake Salary Predictions

Many academic projects train unvalidated ML models that invent salaries on datasets where salary was never disclosed.

Speaker Note: We set out to replace anecdotal opinions with reproducible data science on authentic job postings.

Competitive Advantage

Our Main USP: Data-Driven Career Intelligence

1. Empirical Evidence

Quantitative ranking of skills derived from 12,000 authentic job postings across India to build a high-ROI learning roadmap.

2. Smart Standardization

Deterministic alias mapping for skills (Python, SQL, AWS) and locations (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR) without losing source data.

3. 18-Tier Role Taxonomy

Heuristically groups complex job titles into clean industry role families while preserving original employer titles.

4. Anti-Hallucination Ethics

Strict data ethics: openly disclosing missing salary coverage rather than fabricating synthetic compensation.

Speaker Note: Our USP is Data-Driven Career Intelligence: evidence-based, normalized, categorized, and ethically transparent.

Empirical Results

Quantitative Insights from 12,000 Postings

Most Demanded Skills

1. Python: Over 1,500 mentions (#1 language across ML & Data Engineering)

2. SQL: Foundational requirement across 85% of analytical roles

3. Machine Learning & AWS: Cloud and predictive analytics demand

Employment Geography

1. Bengaluru: 5,093 mentions (~42% of all localized postings)

2. Pune (2,014) & Hyderabad (1,940): Key secondary tech hubs

3. Delhi NCR & Mumbai: Major corporate analytics centers

Job Role Breakdown

Data Engineer: ~27% of dataset (Massive infrastructure demand)

Data Scientist: ~11% (Advanced modeling & statistics)

Business / Data Analyst: ~15% (KPI tracking & dashboards)

Experience & Salary

Average Minimum: 2.8 Years | Average Maximum: 6.4 Years

Fresher (0–2 yrs): ~18% (Demands hands-on project proof)

Salary Disclosed: 0% (Explicitly disclosed as unavailable)

Speaker Note: Python and SQL dominate skills, Bengaluru leads location, and Data Engineering leads roles.
